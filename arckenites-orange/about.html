Building India's Next Tech Workforce

Arckenites was founded to close the gap between classroom learning and what employers actually hire for.

Our Story

From a Small Batch of 12 to 5,000+ Careers Launched

Arckenites started as a single classroom teaching AWS fundamentals to a handful of engineering graduates. Today, we run full-time and weekend programs across Cloud, Development, Data and Security - built by practitioners who still work in the industry.

Every course is redesigned each year against real hiring data, not a fixed syllabus, so what we teach stays aligned with what companies are actually interviewing for.

Our Mission

To make industry-ready IT skills accessible to every student and professional, regardless of their starting background, through mentor-led, project-based training.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted training partner for both learners and hiring companies across India's technology industry.

Our Values

What Guides Every Batch We Run

Integrity
